1. THE IDEA IN SIMPLE WORDS
==============================================================================

Government departments and institutions publish rules as long, complicated
documents: notifications, circulars and orders. Ordinary people cannot easily
understand them, and institutions struggle to keep up when they change.

NiyamSetu AI reads those official documents and does four things:

   1. UNDERSTANDS the rules  (what is required, by whom, by when)
   2. GUIDES people          (what exactly should I do?)
   3. CHECKS applications    (is my application ready to submit?)
   4. TRACKS CHANGES         (when a rule changes, what else is affected?)

Everything is backed by the original document: every answer shows which
document, which page and which clause it came from.

We start with ONE area: education and scholarships. Once it works reliably
there, it can expand to other government services.


==============================================================================
2. THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING
==============================================================================

Five connected problems:

 1. People struggle to understand long official notifications.
    Language is legalistic, documents are long, many are scanned images.

 2. People do not know which rules are current or what has changed.
    A new notification may modify an old one without restating it.

 3. People do not know which schemes they qualify for or what steps to
    follow.
    Benefits go unclaimed because people never find or understand them.

 4. Applications fail because of missing or inconsistent documents.
    Name mismatches, expired certificates, unreadable scans. Often found
    only after submission, sometimes too late.

 5. Institutions struggle to track rule changes, deadlines and affected
    procedures.
    When one rule changes, nobody knows exactly which forms, checklists and
    guidance need updating.

Most existing tools only address problem 1 (a chatbot or a PDF summariser).
NiyamSetu AI addresses the whole chain.


==============================================================================
3. WHAT NIYAMSETU AI IS (AND IS NOT)
==============================================================================

IT IS
  - A platform that turns official documents into understandable, actionable
    and verifiable guidance.
  - One shared rules engine that powers every feature.
  - An evidence-based system: every output points to its official source.
  - A tool with a human review step wherever the AI is unsure.

IT IS NOT
  - A general chatbot.
  - A legal advisor. It does not give legal advice.
  - An official authority. It cannot promise that an application will be
    accepted.
  - A collection of unrelated AI tools. Every module reads from and writes
    to the same rules engine.

Important design stance
  The project is "an intelligent rule-to-action platform", not "a bunch of AI
  features". Fewer, deeper, connected features are stronger than many
  separate ones.

4. THE SIGNATURE INNOVATION: POLICY RIPPLE ENGINE
==============================================================================

This is the feature that makes NiyamSetu AI different from every normal
"chat with a PDF" tool.

THE IDEA
  When one government rule changes, its effects do not stay inside a PDF.
  The platform traces the change through everything that depends on it.

EXAMPLE
  An official scholarship notification changes its income limit.

    Step 1  RuleWeaver detects the change.
    Step 2  The system identifies which schemes and procedures are affected.
    Step 3  JanSetu updates its explanation and eligibility guidance.
    Step 4  FormSathi updates the relevant application checks.
    Step 5  The Eligibility Finder re-evaluates who qualifies.
    Step 6  Deadline and compliance tasks are reviewed for changes.
    Step 7  Affected users and institutions receive an alert.

  Everything goes through human review before it becomes active, and every
  step keeps its source reference.

THE FLOW AT A GLANCE

   Official notification published (new or amended rule)
                     |
                     v
              RuleWeaver
      extracts and identifies the change
                     |
                     v
         Human Review Queue (verify)
                     |
                     v
          POLICY RIPPLE ENGINE
     traces everything affected by the change
        |          |           |            |
        v          v           v            v
    JanSetu    FormSathi   Eligibility   Deadline
    updates    updates     Finder        Tracker
    guidance   checks      rechecks      reviews tasks
        \          |           |            /
         \_________|___________|___________/
                     |
                     v
      Verified updates and change alerts
      (with source references, human review where required)

WHY IT MATTERS
  A normal AI tool answers questions about a notification.
  NiyamSetu AI tracks how that notification affects the actual process.

HONEST NOTE
  This is a proposed capability. We do not claim it works automatically
  until it has been implemented and tested. The first version will be a
  limited, working version focused on one scholarship scheme.

6. FOUNDATION: UNIFIED RULES INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
==============================================================================

The shared brain. Every module uses it. There are no separate AI systems and
no separate copies of the rules.

WHAT IT DOES
  - Reads official PDFs and notifications.
  - Extracts rules, conditions and deadlines.
  - Stores source, page and clause references.
  - Tracks document versions and effective dates.
  - Identifies possible conflicts.
  - Keeps uncertain rules pending human verification.

6.1 DOCUMENT INGESTION
  Input    : text PDFs, scanned PDFs, images.
  Steps    :
    a. Check whether the PDF has selectable text.
    b. If yes, extract the text with page numbers.
    c. If no, run OCR (English first, Telugu where needed).
    d. Record how confident the extraction is, per page.
    e. Store the original file unchanged, plus the extracted text.

6.2 DOCUMENT METADATA
  For every document we record: title, document number, issuing authority,
  publication date, effective date, jurisdiction, subject, whether it says
  it amends or replaces another document, source, and verification status.

6.3 RULE EXTRACTION
  The AI converts text into structured rule records. Each rule has:
    - type (eligibility condition, required document, deadline, obligation,
      benefit amount, procedure step, exception)
    - the condition in words
    - a machine-readable form: field, operator, value
      (example: field = family_income, operator = <=, value = 250000)
    - who it applies to, and for which scheme
    - source document, page and clause
    - extraction confidence
  New rules start as DRAFT. They become ACTIVE only after review.

6.4 VERSIONING AND EFFECTIVE DATES
  Each rule has valid-from and valid-to dates. The system can answer "what is
  the rule today" and "what was the rule on a given date". Old versions are
  kept for history.

6.5 CONFLICT HANDLING
  The system looks at: issuing authority, publication date, effective date,
  jurisdiction, whether one document explicitly amends or supersedes another,
  and whether they cover the same subject.
  If the answer is not clear, the case goes to a human. The AI never decides
  alone that a rule is invalid or that one notification overrides another.

6.6 EVIDENCE RETRIEVAL
  Answers are built only from retrieved rule text. If no supporting evidence
  is found, the system says so instead of guessing.

6.7 ELIGIBILITY EVALUATION
  Conditions that have a field, operator and value are checked by ordinary
  code, not by the AI model. The AI extracts and explains. Code calculates.

7. CORE MODULES
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  MODULE 01: RULEWEAVER  -  "Understand official rules"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who uses it : administrators, reviewers.

What it does
  - Upload circulars and notifications.
  - Extract rules and eligibility conditions.
  - Compare old and new notifications.
  - Highlight added, removed and modified rules.
  - Flag possible conflicts for human review.
  - Generate compliance checklists.

Inputs  : PDF or image documents with their basic details.
Outputs : structured rule list, change report (old vs new), conflict flags,
          draft checklist.

Screens : upload page, document viewer with highlighted rules, comparison
          view, conflict list.

How it works
  Upload -> extract text -> extract rules -> reviewer checks -> rules
  become active -> compare with the earlier version -> change report ->
  hand the change to the Policy Ripple Engine.

Problem solved
  Hidden or changed requirements; unclear which rule is current; slow,
  manual document comparison.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.2  MODULE 02: JANSETU AI  -  "Explain rules and guide people"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who uses it : students, citizens.

What it does
  - Ask questions in English or Telugu.
  - Explain rules in simple language.
  - Show eligibility conditions.
  - Generate personalised step-by-step procedures.
  - Explain required documents.
  - Show the official sources behind every answer.

Inputs  : the user's question, and optional personal details they choose to
          enter (course, category, income range, state).
Outputs : plain-language answer with source citations, personalised
          checklist, step-by-step guide.

Screens : ask-anything box, procedure page with step tracker, document
          explainer.

How it works
  Question -> engine fetches CURRENT approved rules -> AI writes an answer
  using only those rules -> answer shown in the user's language with
  citations.

Rules for this module
  - Only approved (active) rules are used.
  - If a scheme is not in the system, it says so.
  - Every answer shows "Source: document, page, clause".

Problem solved
  People cannot understand procedures or eligibility, especially in their own
  language.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.3  MODULE 03: FORMSATHI AI  -  "Check applications before submission"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who uses it : students, applicants.

What it does
  - Upload application documents.
  - Identify missing documents.
  - Check mismatched information (name, date of birth, parents' names,
    income figures, institution).
  - Check document readability and expiry where applicable.
  - Compare documents with the official requirements.
  - Generate an application-readiness report.

Inputs  : chosen scheme, uploaded documents (for the demo, synthetic ones).
Outputs : readiness report in three groups:
            - Completed requirements
            - Missing requirements
            - Uncertain items needing review
          Every item explains what, why it matters, and which official
          clause requires it.

Screens : scheme selector, upload area, side-by-side field comparison,
          readiness report.

How it works
  Choose scheme -> engine loads the current requirements -> upload documents
  -> extract fields from them -> compare with the requirements and with each
  other -> report.

Critical rule
  The report NEVER says "your application will be accepted". It says "based
  on the requirements we verified, this appears ready" or lists the gaps.
  Final acceptance always belongs to the authority.

Problem solved
  Rejections caused by missing or inconsistent documents, discovered too
  late.


==============================================================================
8. SIGNATURE MODULE: MODULE 04 - POLICY RIPPLE ENGINE
==============================================================================

Purpose
  Show what happens when one government rule changes.

Who uses it : administrators and reviewers (main view); students see the
              results as updated guidance and alerts.

HOW IT WORKS TECHNICALLY
  1. Every rule is stored with LINKS to the things that depend on it:
       - eligibility conditions
       - procedure steps
       - required documents
       - application checks (FormSathi)
       - deadlines and tasks
       - affected users and institutions
  2. When RuleWeaver detects a change in a rule, the engine looks up all
     links of that rule.
  3. It builds an IMPACT REPORT: a list of every linked item with the
     proposed update (old text -> new text).
  4. The report goes to the Human Review Queue.
  5. After approval, the linked items are updated and affected users are
     alerted.

WHAT IT SHOWS
  - A "ripple view": the changed rule in the centre, and everything affected
    around it, grouped by type.
  - For each affected item: what changes, why, and the source clause.
  - Status per item: proposed / approved / applied / rejected.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT (income limit raised from 2.5 lakh to 3.0 lakh)
  Changed rule          : Family income limit 2.5 lakh -> 3.0 lakh
  Affected eligibility  : Income condition for Scheme X
  Affected procedure    : Step 2 "Obtain income certificate" (guidance text)
  Affected documents    : Income certificate validity wording
  Affected app checks   : FormSathi income check threshold
  Affected deadlines    : Re-opening of applications (to be reviewed)
  Affected users        : Students previously marked "not eligible"

FIRST VERSION SCOPE (realistic)
  - One scholarship scheme.
  - Links between rules, eligibility conditions, procedure steps, required
    documents and FormSathi checks.
  - Impact report plus review and approval.
  - Alerts and deadline review added if time allows.

Problem solved
  A rule change normally stays buried in a PDF and nobody knows what else
  must change. This makes the effect visible and traceable.

9. SUPPORTING MODULES
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  MODULE 05: SCHEME ELIGIBILITY FINDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Finds schemes that match information the user chooses to provide.
  - Shows each scheme as: likely eligible / possibly eligible (some
    information missing) / not eligible.
  - Explains the exact conditions behind each result.
  - Connects directly to JanSetu for the steps.
  Problem solved: people do not know which schemes they qualify for.
  Limit: only covers schemes whose documents are in the system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.2  MODULE 06: WHAT-IF SIMULATOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Change an eligibility value or a rule condition.
  - Compare the result before and after.
  - Explain exactly which condition changed the outcome.
  Two uses:
    (a) Student: "What if my family income changes?"
    (b) Administrator: "What if the income limit changes?" (preview before
        a change is approved, works well alongside Policy Ripple)
  Problem solved: people and administrators cannot see how a change affects
  the result.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.3  MODULE 07: SMART DEADLINE & COMPLIANCE TRACKER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Extract deadlines from official rules.
  - Create checklists and reminders.
  - Track pending tasks and completion status.
  - When a rule changes, affected deadlines are flagged for review.
  Problem solved: missed deadlines and incomplete submissions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.4  MODULE 08: GRIEVANCE & RTI ASSISTANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Help draft grievance letters and RTI applications.
  - Reference the relevant official clauses.
  - Explain the general submission procedure.
  Limit: drafts are starting points for the user to review. Not legal
  advice.
  Problem solved: people do not know how to challenge a decision or ask for
  information.


==============================================================================
10. TRUST AND VERIFICATION LAYER
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1  MODULE 09: HUMAN REVIEW QUEUE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Review uncertain extracted rules.
  - Approve or reject proposed rule changes.
  - Resolve possible conflicts.
  Items go to the queue when:
    - extraction confidence is low,
    - two documents seem to conflict,
    - it is unclear whether one document replaces another,
    - a rule or an impact report is about to become active.
  Reviewer actions: approve, edit, reject, add a note.
  Rule: unverified rules must never silently become active.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.2  MODULE 10: TRANSPARENCY & AUDIT DASHBOARD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Show where an answer came from.
  - Show which rule was used.
  - Track changes and review decisions.
  - Record important administrative actions (who did what, when).
  Problem solved: AI answers cannot be trusted without a visible evidence
  trail.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.3  MODULE 11: OFFICIAL SOURCE MONITOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Monitor a selected list of official sources.
  - Identify newly published or updated documents.
  - Send them for verification before use.
  A newly discovered document is NEVER treated as authoritative until it has
  been reviewed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.4  MODULE 12: DOCUMENT AUTHENTICITY & SOURCE CHECK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Check whether a document matches an available official publication.
  - Flag missing or inconsistent source details (document number, date,
    authority).
  - Possible results: "matches an official source", "not found in the
    official sources we monitor", "may be outdated".
  Critical rule: never claim that an automated check proves a document is
  genuine, and never claim it proves one is fake.

13. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE AND TECHNOLOGY STACK
==============================================================================

PRINCIPLE
  One shared backend and one permanent database. No separate AI engine per
  module.

STACK
  Frontend            : Next.js + Tailwind CSS
  Backend             : Next.js server-side APIs (initially)
  Database            : Supabase PostgreSQL
  Authentication      : Supabase Auth
  File storage        : Supabase Storage
  AI                  : Gemini API
  Document processing : PDF text extraction first; OCR for scanned files
  Deployment          : Vercel
  Graph database      : not needed in the first version; normal relational
                        tables with link tables are enough
  Optional later      : vector search (pgvector in Supabase) if keyword and
                        structured search are not enough

LAYERS
  1. Interface        : pages for students and administrators.
  2. Module layer     : RuleWeaver, JanSetu, FormSathi, Policy Ripple and
                        the supporting modules.
  3. Engine layer     : the Unified Rules Intelligence Engine (extraction,
                        versioning, links, evidence, eligibility code).
  4. Data layer       : PostgreSQL tables and file storage.

DESIGN CHOICES
  - Eligibility checks are done by code from structured rules; the AI model
    is used for extraction, explanation and drafting.
  - The AI call is wrapped in one place so the model can be changed later.
  - Prompts, AI outputs and the rules they produced are logged.

15. HOW THE PIECES WORK TOGETHER: A FULL WALKTHROUGH
==============================================================================

Scenario: one scholarship scheme, two versions of its official notification.

 1. UPLOAD
    The administrator uploads the old and the new notification.

 2. EXTRACT
    The engine extracts the text, then the rules, each with page and clause
    references and a confidence score.

 3. COMPARE (RuleWeaver)
    The system highlights what changed, for example the income limit, and
    flags anything unclear.

 4. REVIEW
    A reviewer checks the extracted rules and the detected change in the
    Human Review Queue and approves them.

 5. RIPPLE (Policy Ripple Engine)
    The engine follows the rule's links and produces an impact report: which
    eligibility conditions, procedure steps, required documents and
    application checks are affected, with proposed updates.

 6. APPROVE
    The administrator approves the proposed updates.

 7. GUIDE (JanSetu)
    A student asks, in Telugu or English, whether they qualify. The answer
    uses the updated rule and shows its source.

 8. CHECK (FormSathi)
    The student uploads a synthetic sample application. The readiness report
    uses the updated requirement and lists completed, missing and uncertain
    items with official references.

 9. ALERT AND TRACK
    Affected users get an alert, and deadlines are reviewed.

10. AUDIT
    The dashboard shows the evidence: which document, which rule, who
    approved what, and when.

Key message: one connected demonstration, not eight unrelated features.

16. SECURITY, PRIVACY AND RESPONSIBLE AI RULES
==============================================================================

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
  1. Every important AI-generated rule or answer keeps its source document
     and page reference.
  2. Unverified rules must not silently become active.
  3. The AI never decides alone that a rule is legally invalid or that one
     notification overrides another. Unclear cases go to human review.
  4. If the system has no evidence, it says it does not know.
  5. Readiness reports never promise acceptance.
  6. Authenticity checks never claim to prove a document is genuine or fake.
  7. Newly discovered documents are not authoritative until reviewed.
  8. Eligibility calculations are done by code, not by the AI model.
  9. The Policy Ripple Engine is described as a working feature only after it
     has been implemented and tested.
 10. Use synthetic personal documents in the demonstration.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
  - Role-based access on every page and API.
  - Users can view and delete their own uploaded documents.
  - Collect only the personal details that are needed, and only when the user
    chooses to provide them.
  - Encrypt data in transit; use the access controls of the database and
    storage provider.
  - Do not store Aadhaar numbers or biometric data.
  - Keep API keys and secrets in environment variables, never in code.
  - Where real personal data is ever used, follow India's Digital Personal
    Data Protection Act (consent, purpose limitation, deletion).

DISCLAIMER SHOWN IN THE PRODUCT
  "This is guidance based on documents in the system. It is not an official
  decision or legal advice. Please confirm with the issuing authority."


==============================================================================
17. TECHNICAL CHALLENGES AND SOLUTIONS
==============================================================================

Outdated notifications treated as current
   -> versioning with effective dates; only active rules used.

AI hallucination
   -> answers only from retrieved rules, with citations; "I could not find
      this" when evidence is missing.

Conflicting instructions
   -> conflict flags and human review.

Poor PDF or scan quality
   -> OCR, per-page confidence, manual correction by a reviewer.

Telugu text quality
   -> test early with real documents; allow manual correction.

Rules hidden in tables
   -> layout-aware extraction where possible, plus review.

Rule-to-process links being wrong
   -> links are created and reviewed by a person at first; ripple results
      always go through approval.

Too much scope for one developer
   -> strict priorities (section 18); build one connected demo well.

Proving accuracy
   -> small test set with known correct answers (section 21).

22. RISKS AND MITIGATION
==============================================================================

Scope too large for one developer
   -> one scheme, one connected demo, strict priority list.

Wrong or invented AI output
   -> evidence grounding, human review, visible confidence.

Telugu extraction errors
   -> test early, manual correction.

Ripple links incomplete or wrong
   -> reviewer approval of links and impact reports.

Dependence on one AI provider
   -> keep the AI call in one place so it can be swapped.

Privacy problems
   -> synthetic demo data, minimal collection, deletion option.

Over-claiming in the presentation
   -> describe only what has been built and tested; show limits honestly.

Running out of AI-assistant usage
   -> plan first, small focused instructions, reuse the shared engine.


==============================================================================
23. WHAT WAS REMOVED AND WHY
==============================================================================

FUNDWATCH (removed completely)
  It analysed budgets and spending, which is a different kind of problem
  (numeric data analysis, not rule understanding). It needed different data
  and carried a risk of accusing without proof. It does not strengthen the
  rule-to-action story.

EMERGENCY ASSIST (removed completely)
  It involved sensitive health and biometric data, legal approvals and
  integrations that cannot be done responsibly in this project, and it has
  nothing to do with understanding rules.

IMPACT & CHANGE MONITORING AS A SEPARATE MODULE
  Merged into the Policy Ripple Engine, which does this job in a more
  powerful and connected way.

Principle behind the cuts
  Do not add five or six unrelated AI tools. That makes the project look
  bigger, not more innovative. The strength is in the connection between
  modules.

25. GLOSSARY
==============================================================================

Notification / circular / order : official document announcing a rule or a
                                  change.
Rule                            : one requirement or condition taken from a
                                  document (for example an income limit).
Version                         : a rule or document as it stood at a
                                  particular time.
Effective date                  : the date from which a rule applies.
Citation                        : the document, page and clause an answer
                                  came from.
Human review                    : a person checks and approves what the AI
                                  produced.
Ripple / impact                 : everything that must change because one
                                  rule changed.
Readiness report                : a check of an application against the
                                  verified requirements; not a promise of
                                  acceptance.
Synthetic data                  : made-up sample data used instead of real
                                  personal documents.
RTI                             : Right to Information application.
OCR                             : technology that reads text from scanned
                                  images.

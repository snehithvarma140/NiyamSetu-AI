# 🇮🇳 NiyamSetu AI
### Bridging Government Services and Citizens Through Intelligent AI

**Understand the rules. Find your benefits. Complete your applications.**

NiyamSetu AI is an AI-powered GovTech platform designed to make government schemes, rules, notifications, and public services easier for citizens to understand and access.

The platform brings citizens, government departments, and approved service organisations together through a unified intelligent system. It aims to simplify complex government information, assist citizens with applications, and help government departments understand the impact of policy and rule changes.

---

## 🚩 The Problem

Citizens often face difficulties accessing government services because:

- Government information is scattered across multiple websites and documents.
- Scheme eligibility and application requirements can be difficult to understand.
- Citizens may not know which documents are required.
- Application procedures and status updates can be confusing.
- Changes in government rules are not always easy to identify.
- Finding reliable nearby government assistance can be challenging.

## 💡 Our Solution

NiyamSetu AI provides a unified platform where citizens can discover schemes, understand government rules, receive application guidance, and access relevant public services.

It also provides government departments with AI-assisted tools to organise official information, review extracted rules, and analyse the possible effects of policy changes.

---

## 🤖 Core AI Modules

### 1. RuleWeaver AI
Transforms official government notifications and documents into structured, searchable rule information.

- Extracts relevant rules and requirements.
- Organises information by schemes and departments.
- Maintains source references and document evidence.
- Supports human review and approval.

### 2. JanSetu AI
An intelligent citizen-facing assistant that helps people understand government services.

- Answers questions about schemes and rules.
- Explains eligibility and procedures in simple language.
- Provides references to verified official sources.
- Helps citizens navigate relevant services.

### 3. FormSathi AI
An AI-powered application guidance assistant.

- Explains application procedures step by step.
- Identifies required and optional documents.
- Helps users understand application forms.
- Supports application preparation and document readiness.

### 4. Policy Ripple Engine
An AI-assisted policy impact analysis system.

- Compares changes between government rules.
- Identifies directly linked schemes and procedures.
- Highlights potentially affected requirements.
- Supports evidence-based policy review.

**Unified Rules Intelligence Engine:** Connects the four modules through a shared structure for managing official rules, source documents, verification, and approved information.

NiyamSetu AI is designed around three connected portals and one shared intelligence layer.

       CITIZEN     GOVERNMENT   ORGANISATION
       PORTAL        PORTAL        PORTAL
          |            |            |
          +------------+------------+
                       |
          UNIFIED RULES INTELLIGENCE
                   ENGINE
                       |
       +---------------+---------------+
       |               |               |
   RULEWEAVER       JANSETU         FORMSATHI
       |                               |
       +-------- POLICY RIPPLE --------+
                       |

                       |

The architecture is intended to support secure information sharing, role-based access, verified government sources, and modular expansion.

## 🔐 Security, Reliability and Trust

NiyamSetu AI is designed with the following principles:

- Official sources for authoritative government information.
- Human approval before extracted rules become approved guidance.
- Source references to support AI-generated answers.
- Role-based access for citizens, departments, administrators, and organisations.
- Protection of private citizen documents and personal information.
- Server-side handling of private API credentials.
- Audit trails for sensitive administrative actions.
- Clear distinction between prepared applications and officially submitted applications.

AI-generated information should not replace official government decisions or approvals.
